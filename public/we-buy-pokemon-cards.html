How it works at the counter

Evolutions TCG is a physical storefront at 100 Tall Pines Ave., Suite 3 — the buying happens face to face, at the counter, during posted store hours. No shipping labels, no online instant-offer algorithms, no auction fees quoted sight unseen. You show what you have, the Mannings look at it, and you both decide there and then whether the shop buys it or helps you find a buyer for it.

Two honest notes, because they are not published yet: the shop has not posted a buylist or published rates anywhere public, so pricing is a counter conversation, not a webpage you can read in advance — and the announcement says the shop “buys people's cards or helps them sell them,” which covers two different arrangements (outright purchase versus helping you sell). Ask which one fits your cards before anything changes hands.

Questions people actually ask

Does Evolutions TCG buy Pokémon cards in Longview?

Yes — buying people's cards, or helping them sell them, is part of how the owners described the shop when it opened. It's their words to the Longview News-Journal, November 2026, not our marketing.

How do I get my cards looked at?

Bring them to the counter at 100 Tall Pines Ave., Suite 3 during the posted hours. Prefer to ask first? Use the contact form with what you've got — game, set, condition, how many — and someone will follow up.

Do you buy other trading card games?

The shop chose deliberately to be Pokémon-only, per the owners — it's part of how they built the kid-friendly atmosphere. Ask at the counter about anything specific you're trying to sell.

Can I sell a graded or investment card?

The owners have been in the hobby since childhood and the shop is collector-facing. Bring the slab or card in, or describe it in the form. Whatever grading language comes up, it starts as a face-to-face conversation.

Do I need an appointment?

No appointment is listed in anything the shop has announced. Posted hours are the posted hours — walk in during them.

Everything on this page comes from what the shop has made public (Longview News-Journal coverage of the opening, November 2026). Anything not announced — buylists, rates, consignment terms — is deliberately not invented here.

Wondering if your cards are worth anything?

Send a short description through the form or just stop by during posted hours. Worst case: you learn something and buy a pack on the way out.
