The people · Longview, TX

Bobby & Jordan Manning

Married. Collecting since they were kids. They built the card shop Longview never had — on purpose, with a point of view.

Home / Story

Why this shop exists

Bobby and Jordan Manning have been part of the Pokémon hobby since childhood, and when they decided to open Evolutions TCG, they described it as building the shop they remember wanting as kids. The store announced as a Longview opening in November 2026 — East Texas's first dedicated Pokémon shop, in their telling to the Longview News-Journal.

Their decisions carry that through. The shop is Pokémon-only on purpose: other trading card games, in Jordan's telling to the News-Journal, don't suit the kid-friendly atmosphere they wanted, so they left them out. Jordan is a former nurse. Bobby also operates God's Money Gold & Silver Bullion in Tyler — a collector-adjacent operation, from a couple who think about cards as collectibles as much as toys.

“We haven't had a slow day since we opened.”

— Bobby Manning, to the Longview News-Journal (Nov 2026)

What they've said the shop is for

Per the opening coverage: selling singles, booster packs and toys; buying people's cards or helping them sell them; and planning tournament events for the community. Everything on this site sticks to those announcements — the hours, the address, and the story here are exactly what the couple has said in public, and nothing has been invented to fill gaps.
