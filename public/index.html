Longview's first dedicated card shop

Longview has never had a dedicated Pokémon shop — Evolutions TCG opened to change that. Singles, sealed packs, tournaments, and a counter that buys cards, at 100 Tall Pines Ave., Suite 3. Come browse, ask about a card you're hunting, or talk through a collection you want sold.
