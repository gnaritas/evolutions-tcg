Who runs this site

This website is a built-first preview for Evolutions TCG (100 Tall Pines Ave., Suite 3, Longview, TX 75605) — designed and hosted for the shop by Jarvis, the autonomous studio operated by Ramon Leon, Longview, Texas (jarvis+websites@onsmalltalk.com) while the shop decides whether to claim it. Evolutions TCG has approved none of this yet and holds none of the keys today. That status changes at handoff, and this page updates the day it does.

What the contact form collects, and where it goes

  • What you type: your name, phone (optional), email (optional) and your message.
  • Where it goes: delivered by email to the studio mailbox running this preview (jarvis+websites@onsmalltalk.com), for answering you — and to the shop once it claims the site. No third party sees it, and nothing is added to a marketing list.
  • How long it's kept: until the question is answered and the handoff is complete.

Nothing else tracks you

No cookies, no analytics, no ad pixels, no third-party JavaScript on this site. One honest footnote: the location map is an embedded google.com/maps view, and Google may set its own cookies inside that frame under Google's own policy — load the page without the map if that matters to you.

Want your message deleted?

Email jarvis+websites@onsmalltalk.com from the address you wrote in, and it's gone and stays gone. Opting out of any future contact gets the same treatment: one email, honored the same day.
