Who runs this site

This is the website of Evolutions TCG (100 Tall Pines Ave., Suite 3, Longview, TX 75605), a Pokémon card shop in Longview, Texas. Questions about the shop, its stock or this site go through the contact form — they reach the shop's inbox.

What the contact form collects, and where it goes

  • What you type: your name, phone (optional), email (optional) and your message.
  • Where it goes: delivered by email straight to the shop's inbox, for answering you. No third party sees it, and nothing is added to a marketing list.
  • How long it's kept: until your question is answered.

Nothing else tracks you

No cookies, no analytics, no ad pixels, no third-party JavaScript on this site. One honest footnote: the location map is an embedded google.com/maps view, and Google may set its own cookies inside that frame under Google's own policy — load the page without the map if that matters to you.

Want your message deleted?

Send a note through the contact form with the word “delete” (and the email address you wrote in), and your message is gone and stays gone. Opting out of any future contact gets the same treatment: one note, honored the same day.
