Singles, booster packs & toys

Three shelves, one franchise, on purpose. Here's what the shop announced it stocks and what to expect when you walk onto the floor at 100 Tall Pines Ave.

Home / Singles, packs & toys

The three things on the shelves

Singles one at a time

Looking for one specific card rather than a mystery box? The shop carries singles — announced at opening. What's in stock moves, so the counter is the inventory.

Booster packs sealed

Packs to rip. The announcement lists packs among what the shop sells; set prices are shelf-side, not published online.

Toys for younger trainers

The Mannings built this shop around the kid-friendly atmosphere they remembered wanting as kids — and toys for the youngest collectors are part of that.

Pokémon only — deliberately

Other card shops chase every franchise under the sun. Evolutions TCG said at opening that it chose to focus on Pokémon specifically to keep the shop kid-friendly, because "not every type of trading card game suits that atmosphere," in the couple's telling to the News-Journal. That's a positioning decision, not an oversight — and it's why the shelves look the way they do.

Prices and stock levels are not published by the shop anywhere public as of this writing, so this page doesn't invent them. Shelf tags and the counter are the source of truth.

Questions from the counter

What can I find at Evolutions TCG in Longview?

Pokémon singles, booster packs and toys — the three things the shop's opening announcement lists.

Do you publish prices?

Not publicly, so far. Shelf tags and the counter decide, and this preview won't guess on the shop's behalf.

Why only Pokémon and not other card games?

It was a deliberate choice by the owners, announced at opening, to protect the kid-friendly atmosphere the shop was built for.

Are kids welcome without an adult?

Kids are the shop's whole point. No supervision rule has been announced — for a young trainer out trading alone, ask at the counter.

Hunting for one specific card?

Send the card name through the form — if the shop stocks it, the counter is two minutes from telling you. The form reaches the studio inbox running this preview today and the shop once they claim it.
